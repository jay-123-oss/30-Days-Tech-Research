## 🌟 About The Project

**30-Days-Tech-Research** is a personal research challenge where I explore something new every day for 30 consecutive days.

The goal is not simply to search for information.

The goal is to:

- 🔎 Discover something new
- 📚 Understand how it works
- 🧠 Analyze the underlying concept
- 🔬 Research existing solutions
- 💡 Identify limitations and opportunities
- 🚀 Develop my own observations and ideas
- 📝 Document everything
- 🌐 Build a public knowledge base

Every day will produce a new research document.

At the end of 30 days, this repository will contain **30 days of technical research and ideas**.

---

# 🎯 Challenge Objective

The main objective of this challenge is to develop a habit of:

> **Research → Understand → Question → Experiment → Document → Improve**

Instead of only consuming technology, this challenge focuses on understanding **why technologies work, how they work, what problems they solve, and what could be improved.**
